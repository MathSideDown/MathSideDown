MATHSIDE DOWN

Escape the Upside Down

You are trapped in the Upside Down — a dark mirror of Hawkins. Solve math challenges to open doors, avoid the Demogorgon, and reach the exit before time runs out. Earn extra hearts at Mystery Stations.

HOW TO PLAY

Move with W A S D, the arrow keys, or swipe on a phone. Step on a glowing rift to answer a math question: right opens the way and scores points, wrong costs a heart. Star stations give an extra heart. The maze scrolls as you move. Correct answers add time. The Demogorgon gets faster and smarter with each level — Nursery is clumsy, College is ruthless — so reach the green exit before the timer ends.
